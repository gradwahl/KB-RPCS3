## Bottom line for GTA IV

**No substantiated drop-in FPS win found in the audited repository/forks.** More productive than transplanting the firmware-specific pool is adapting the parent **PPU/SPU/RSX CPU-time overlay measurement**, testing whether its PPU/RSX attribution agrees with native thread data, and following the patch0066 high-traffic measurements with exact register/value-change and *exclusive CPU-cost* sampling. In the indoor run, 0x1700-0x17FF represented 107,352 sampled words with only 62 direct handlers: that is diagnostic evidence for where to inspect decode/latching, not proof that optimizing it improves GTA IV FPS.
